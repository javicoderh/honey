# Jardín de miel

Arte original generado con la herramienta integrada image_gen para la portada de Casa Miel. Archivo: `jardin-de-miel.png`, 1536 × 1024 px. Es una interpretación artística, no una fotografía documental de los productores ni de sus apiarios.

## Prompt utilizado

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: wide 3:2 painted hero artwork for a Chilean honey storefront. Create an original fine-art dreamscape blending Roberto Matta's biomorphic surrealism and impossible fluid spaces, lyrical hand-painted anime background sensibility, and post-impressionist broken brush strokes. A pleasure-filled, contemplative living garden of honey and bees: small expressive golden bees drifting around a luminous amber honey pool, fragments of cherry blossoms and suggested Chilean hills forming and dissolving into creamy empty space. Scene emerges impressionistically rather than detailed literal rendering. Floating organic islands and transparent curved ribbons, delicately suggested honeycomb geometry, a tiny distant anime-style beekeeper silhouette in the lower right landscape (not a portrait). Warm amber, ochre and butter yellow with cool teal, moss green, coral and dusty lilac accents. Oil and gouache on textured ivory paper; tactile lively brushwork, confident fine dark contours in selected areas, sophisticated rather than childish. Asymmetric composition: airy ivory upper left and left third intentionally mostly empty, concentrated painterly worlds at center-right and lower right, large breathing spaces throughout, no hard frame. Gentle light, poetic calm and wonder, honey visibly connects the abstract art to the shop. Landscape image, high quality. No text, no letters, no UI, no watermark, no logos, no photorealistic packaging, no dense all-over pattern.

## Integración

`src/pages/Home.jsx` presenta el lienzo con texto y navegación HTML independientes. `src/styles/home-art.css` controla encuadre, contraste y disposición móvil. La imagen se carga prioritariamente como recurso principal del primer pantallazo. En móvil se sitúa después del texto para conservar legibilidad y mostrar la escena completa con suficiente espacio.

La composición busca despertar interés y facilitar el acceso al catálogo; su efecto sobre ventas debe medirse con comportamiento real de usuarios.
